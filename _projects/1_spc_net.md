SPC-Net combines SAM and CLIP using a multi-prompt bank with element-wise max aggregation for robust RGB salient object detection.

- Strong performance on five RGB benchmarks
- Competitive results against RGB-D methods on SIP using RGB-only input
- Focus on practical performance under constrained compute
