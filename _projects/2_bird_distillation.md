This work distills knowledge from a Swin-Base teacher to a GCViT student model for improved fine-grained classification.

- Student accuracy improved from 86.76% to 88.16% on CUB-200-2011
- Ensemble reached 90.42% on CUB-200-2011 and 89.36% on NABirds
- Ablations performed under a single 6GB GPU setup
